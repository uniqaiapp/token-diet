# Code of conduct

Be direct and be kind. Review the work, not the person. Assume the other side is busy and acting in good faith. Disagreements about rules and numbers are settled by a test or a measurement, not by volume.

Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory remarks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or discussions. Report problems to support@uniqai.app; maintainers may edit, lock or remove content and block repeat offenders.
